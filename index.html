This year, we're being forged

A call to every young person to come through fire refined, strengthened and shaped for purpose.

PYLR is Lagos Province Seven Teens Affairs' gathering for teenagers and children under the RCCG Directorate of Teens and Children Education. This year's edition, themed Forged, brings teens and children from parishes across the province together for a morning of worship, ministration and impartation.

Event details

VenueRCCG Fruitful Vine, 6–7 Ishmaila Ajibade, Nobex, Ikotun, Lagos
DateSaturday, 3rd October 2026
Time9:00 AM prompt
Fee₦1,000 — compulsory
Open toTeens & children, LP7 parishes

What to expect

A morning built around worship, the ministration of the word and fellowship — come with an open heart, ready to be shaped.

  • Worship & ministration — centered on this year's theme, Forged
  • Fellowship — teens and children from across LP7 parishes, together in one place
  • On-site registration — bring your ₦1,000 registration fee on the day if you haven't paid ahead
